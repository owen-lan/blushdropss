Questions,
answered.

Is this safe?

Yes. Scanning is read-only — no wallet connection required. Claiming requires one signature, and the entire flow is executed by a public smart contract you can inspect on Etherscan.

Which airdrops do you support?

UNI is live. ENS, 1inch, Arbitrum, Optimism and dYdX are being indexed next. Follow the ledger for updates.

What if I never claimed?

Then this is free money you forgot about. The distributor contract still holds it. We just help you find and unlock it.

Do you hold my tokens?

Never. Everything happens in a single transaction. The tokens move from the distributor to your wallet, and the transaction ends. We don't custody, hold, or delay anything.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. You only pay the network gas fee for the claim transaction. If there's nothing to claim, you pay nothing.
