FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Is this safe?

Yes. Scanning is read-only — no wallet connection required. Claiming requires one signature, and the entire flow is executed by a public smart contract you can inspect on Etherscan.

Which airdrops do you support?

UNI is live. ENS, 1inch, Arbitrum, Optimism, and dYdX are being indexed next.

What if I never claimed?

Then this is free money you forgot about. The distributor contract still holds it. We just help you find and unlock it.

Do you hold my tokens?

Never. Everything happens in a single transaction. Tokens move from the distributor directly to your wallet, and the transaction ends.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. You only pay network gas. Swaps include a 0.5% service fee. Buying crypto includes the provider's standard fee.

Can I swap any token?

Yes — any ERC-20 with liquidity. If a token isn't in our search list, paste its contract address directly.
